This guide catalogues roughly 38 distinct Grogu-depicting Funko Pop! vinyl SKUs, plus companion Bitty Pop, Mystery Mini, and pin products, spanning the numbering range #368 through the high #830s across a single continuous Star Wars Pop! line. Grogu does not appear in Ahsoka or Skeleton Crew, and no figures tied to either show were found. § 01

How This Checklist Is Organized

Star Wars Funko Pop!s share one continuous numbering line across the entire franchise — a single sequential counter that spans every Star Wars sub-property Funko has ever produced a Pop! for. That means Pop numbers in this checklist are not sequential within a show or season; a Grogu figure numbered in the 400s might sit right next to an unrelated Obi-Wan or Ahsoka Tano figure in Funko's own internal catalog. This checklist instead organizes entries chronologically by show/era — the order collectors actually encounter them — while preserving the exact Pop number for each figure as researched.

Research was conducted July 30, 2026. Every figure where the Pop number, exact release date, or price could not be independently confirmed is flagged inline in its section table, rather than presented as settled fact. A full accounting of sourcing confidence, by category, is in § 14.

Scope note: this guide covers figures, minis, and pins that depict Grogu specifically. Related sub-line products that don't depict Grogu himself (e.g. Clang, the Mercenary Guard Droid, Zeb Orrelios in the Razor Crest — all part of the 2026 movie wave) are named for context in § 10 but excluded from the checklist proper.

§ 02

§ 09

Ahsoka and Skeleton Crew — No Grogu Figures Found

Grogu does not appear as a character in Ahsoka (2023). The only Grogu-adjacent product tied to that show's branding is a Loungefly-style "Ahsoka & Grogu" 4-pack enamel pin set (2 Ahsoka pins, 2 Grogu pins, ≈1.5in each). This is a pin product, not a vinyl Pop! figure — it packages the two characters together for cross-promotional purposes rather than depicting an in-show appearance. See § 12 for the full pin listing.[22][23]

Grogu also does not appear in Skeleton Crew (2024–2025). Skeleton Crew has its own separate Funko Pop line (e.g. the "KB" character, #701), with no Grogu tie-in found.[24][25]

The Mandalorian and Grogu film reportedly contains a "hidden" nod/cameo referencing Skeleton Crew, but that is a film Easter egg, not evidence Grogu appears in the Skeleton Crew series itself.[26]

This is a negative finding, not exhaustive absence proof — but it is consistent across every source checked in this research. No Funko Pop! Town set featuring Grogu was found either.

See § 14, "Not Found / Explicitly Ruled Out"

The Mandalorian and Grogu (2026 Film)

§ 11

Bitty Pop! Multipacks — Grogu as Mystery "Chase" Bonus

Funko's Bitty Pop! Star Wars: The Mandalorian 4-packs (Series 1–4) each include three named 0.9in mini-figures plus one mystery bonus figure. Grogu variants are used as that mystery pull, which makes them the closest thing to a designated "chase" variant anywhere in the Grogu lineup — standard-format Grogu Pop!s do not appear to have a designated chase variant as of this research.[37][38]

§ 13

Most Sought-After / Highest-Value Pieces

Based on convention/retailer exclusivity, limited production runs, and collector price-guide estimates gathered throughout this research, the standout pieces are:

1. Grogu, Diamond Collection #368Star Wars Celebration 2022, limited edition of 5,000 — hard production cap plus convention-only distribution.[12]
2. The Child with Pendant #398NYCC 2020 exclusive — one of the higher price-guide estimates (≈$65) among standard-scale Grogu Pops.[4][5]
3. Grogu with Rancor #587Entertainment Earth exclusive, 10" Jumbo — large-scale sculpt restricted to a single retail channel.[14][15]
4. Dark Trooper w/ Grogu, GITD #488Entertainment Earth exclusive — scene-specific glow-in-the-dark deco tied to a fan-favorite Season 2 moment.[11]
5. "Grogu Moving" Loungefly PinC2E2 2024, limited edition of 700 — a very small production run, though a pin rather than a vinyl Pop.[41]
6. Bitty Pop! Hyper Rare Grogu pulls1-in-6 pull rate — the closest thing to a chase variant in the line, valuable to collectors completing blind-box sets.[37][38]

No hard current secondary-market sale-price data (e.g. recent completed eBay/StockX sales) was found for most of these pieces. This "most valuable" designation is based on production scarcity and exclusivity signals from retailer/collector sites — not verified recent transaction prices — with the exception of the ≈$65 (#398) and ≈$30 (IG-12 with Grogu Deluxe #720, § 07) price-guide estimates cited directly.

§ 14

Sourcing Confidence Assessment

This research draws on collector checklist sites, official manufacturer pages, primary retailer/exclusive-seller listings, and toy-press reporting on official reveals — not a single authoritative catalog, since Funko does not publish one. Confidence varies meaningfully by claim, and is broken out explicitly below rather than presented as uniform.

Well-Supported
Corroborated across independent sources

The core Mandalorian Season 1–2 numbered lineup (#368–#498) is corroborated across multiple independent checklist sites — pop-figures.com, CardboardConnection, and funkypriceguide.com — with consistent numbering.[1][2][9]

Confirmed via primary retail source

The Book of Boba Fett figures (#584, #587) and the Season 3 IG-12 Deluxe (#720) are confirmed via Entertainment Earth's own product pages — a primary retail source, not an aggregator.[11][13][14][18]

Confirmed via official reveal reporting

The 2026 movie wave's headline figures (#818, AT-RT #837) and pricing are confirmed via Funko's official Toy Fair 2026 reveal, as reported by Laughing Place and corroborated by Collider and TechRadar.[27][28][32]

Thinly Sourced or Unconfirmed
Single-source lead, not independently verified

"Grogu with Crest" #819 rests on a single retailer search-result snippet (bigbadtoystore.com) that could not be directly fetched to verify — treat that number as a lead, not a confirmed fact.[31]

Confirmed SKU, unconfirmed Pop #

"Grogu with Snack (Flocked)" and "Grogu in Pram" could not be matched to a confirmed numbered-line SKU, even though both are confirmed as real Funko.com products.[27][29]

Possible duplicate / data error

The #575 "Grogu at Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade" entry could not be reconciled against the #474/#475 pair from the same event, and may be a duplicate or data error in one source.[1]

Inconsistent across listings

The release year for the "Impressions"/Samurai #772 figure is inconsistent across retailer listings (2024 vs. 2025 appear in different listings).[20][21]

Not Found / Explicitly Ruled Out
Negative finding, consistent across sources

No Grogu-specific standard Pop! vinyl figure tied to Ahsoka or Skeleton Crew exists, because Grogu does not appear as a character in either show — consistent across every source checked, though this is a negative finding rather than exhaustive absence proof. No Funko Pop! Town set featuring Grogu was found.[22][23][24][25][26]

Currency of This List
Snapshot, not a permanent record

This research reflects the state of the Funko Star Wars line as of late July 2026. Funko continuously adds new SKUs, particularly around the recently released Mandalorian and Grogu film, so this checklist should be treated as a snapshot rather than a permanently complete list — new GameStop, Hot Topic, Box Lunch, or SDCC-2026-adjacent exclusives tied to the film may not yet be reflected here.
