The Wandering Inn
Book One, Part One · pirateaba · HarperCollins · Paperback · Released August 11, 2026
One of the longest-running and most acclaimed LitRPG web serials — running online continuously since July 2016 — has entered physical print for the first time. It was previously available only as ebook, audiobook, or free web serial. HarperCollins is releasing it in four parts on a monthly cadence: Book One Pt. 1 (Aug 11), Pt. 2 (Sept 22), Book Two Pt. 1 (Oct 20), Pt. 2 (Nov 10) — all 2026.
Per the author's own announcement, this is a rewritten and re-edited edition prepared specifically for the print deal — not a straight reprint of the web text.
Notable change for existing free-serial readers: as part of the traditional-publishing tradeoff, Volumes 1–2 are now gated behind the print/ebook release rather than freely available on the web serial site.
Four-part monthly rollout as announced by HarperCollins/pirateaba. Only Part One has released as of this cycle; the remaining three are tracked for future cycles, not assumed.
Verified via: wanderinginn.com (author announcement) ScreenRant Walmart listing McNally Robinson listing

Patch Verification Notes
Active Effects — Sourcing & Scope
Scope Lock · Physical Only
This tracker covers paperback/hardcover releases only. Ebook- and audio-only titles, however notable, are explicitly out of scope for this recurring task.
Method · Retailer-Verified
Every confirmed item above was checked against a live publisher or retailer product page (Aethon, Target, Walmart, Amazon, McNally Robinson), not inferred from a release announcement alone — announcements and actual print availability commonly diverge in this genre.
Thin · Publisher Coverage
Mountaindale Press, Wraithmarked Creative, Royal Road author-direct storefronts, Tapas Media, and Portal Books were not conclusively checked this cycle due to stale/cached listing pages — a gap to close next run, not a finding that these publishers had no releases.
