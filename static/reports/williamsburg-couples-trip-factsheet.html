Sunday — Morning, Then Afternoon Departure
FN–07
Morning
Anchor
Historic Jamestowne — Archaeology Walking Tour of James Fort
11:00am or 2:00pm tour · led by a Preservation Virginia archaeologist · dual-sourced via Historic Jamestowne's own site + NPS's own event page
Walks the ACTIVE excavation inside the real James Fort footprint — distinct from Jamestown Settlement, the museum/replica attraction. A strong, genuine match for Ashe's archaeology background: real dig work and current-season finds, not reenactment. Gate opens 8:45am, visitor center/ticketing 9am, Archaearium & museum store 9:30am. Suggested shape: arrive ≈9am, browse the Archaearium/visitor center, take the 11am tour (≈45min–1hr), leaving midday free for lunch and the drive home.
⚠ Seasonal — the daily walking tour runs April–October only. Re-check the schedule if this trip lands outside that window.
Alternative / backup if timing doesn't work or a nature-forward morning is preferred: York River State Park (≈11 miles from Colonial Williamsburg, trails, Taskinas Creek paddling in season) — verified via official Virginia DCR page.

Lodging — Marriott / Bonvoy Lens
Williamsburg Lodge
Verified Pick
Autograph Collection (Marriott) · Inside Colonial Williamsburg
A historic 1939 Rockefeller-era lodge, still owned and operated by the Colonial Williamsburg Foundation's for-profit arm, now part of Marriott's Autograph Collection. Sits inside Colonial Williamsburg itself — extremely well-located, walking distance to Merchants Square — verified via Marriott's own site and Colonial Williamsburg's own site.
Honest note: since this trip is Williamsburg-only, the research didn't surface a second Marriott/MVC/Interval International property to compare it against the way the earlier Richmond+Williamsburg version did — the Lodge is the one strong, well-verified pick for this specific weekend.
