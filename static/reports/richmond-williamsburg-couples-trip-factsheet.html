The Route
Strip Map — Alexandria to the Historic Triangle and BackNot to scale
Reading this sheet: Richmond goes first because it's the shorter hop from Alexandria (≈1h45m), leaving Williamsburg — and the longer drive home (≈2h45m) — for the back half of the trip. Flipping the order is equally reasonable if it suits logistics better. The Richmond–Williamsburg leg itself has no confirmed drive time in the source research and is flagged rather than guessed at — it's commonly a short hop via I-64 E, but that specific figure wasn't independently verified here. Smithfield is drawn as a dashed branch off Williamsburg because it's a genuine extension, not a stop on the core 2-day plan.
Day 1 — Richmond
Morning
VMFA — Virginia Museum of Fine Arts
200 N. Arthur Ashe Blvd · Free general admission, permanent collection · Open 365 days/yr · Mon–Tue & Sat–Sun 10am–5pm, Wed–Fri 10am–9pm
A relaxed, no-cost anchor for the morning — free admission and long Wed–Fri hours make it easy to linger without watching the clock.
Midday
Carytown
≈1-mile retail/dining strip, south end of the Museum District
Lunch, then browse. Bookstores: Fountain Bookstore, Black Swan Books, Shelf Life Books, and Abi's (a coffee-shop/bookstore hybrid) — bbgb, the children's specialty shop, is skipped on this couple's-trip version. Coffee: Sugar and Twine, Griffin Coffee & Bakery, Blanchard's Coffee, or Lamplighter Coffee Roasters. The historic Byrd Theatre, with its Wurlitzer organ, is also here — worth a look even if not catching a show.
Afternoon
Extend Carytown, or Scott's Addition
Either / Or
The Veil Brewing Co. · 1509B Belleville St · Mon–Wed 11am–9pm, Thu 11am–10pm, Fri–Sat 11am–11pm, Sun 11am–8pm
No kid-nap-schedule pressure this trip — a genuinely unhurried pace is on the table. Keep wandering Carytown, or head to Scott's Addition for The Veil.

Day 2 — Williamsburg
Morning
Colonial Williamsburg
Open 365 days/yr · most historic trades/sites 10am–5pm, evening programming extends later · single-day adult ticket $37.50 · Art Museums admission free
2026 is the 250th anniversary of independence — expect extra programming and heavier crowds, so build in flexibility. Without a toddler's bedtime to manage, a couple's trip can actually take advantage of the evening programming.
Alternative: Jamestown Settlement — open year-round 9am–5pm except major holidays; living-history recreated ships, fort, and Powhatan village.
Midday
Precarious Beer Project or Gabriel Archer Tavern
Either / Or
Precarious Beer Project · 110 S Henry St, one block from the Historic Area · beer-hall format, taqueria, smash-burger bar, arcade games
Alternative: Gabriel Archer Tavern at Williamsburg Winery (largest winery in Virginia, 300-acre property, est. 1985) — daily tastings, 30–45 min tours; satellite tasting room at 427 Duke of Gloucester St, Merchants Square.
Afternoon
Busch Gardens, a brewery/winery, or more Colonial Williamsburg
Pick One
A genuinely three-way fork for a couple's trip: Busch Gardens Williamsburg becomes a viable full option without a 14-month-old's nap schedule to work around. Or continue the brewery/winery thread — Alewerks Brewing Company (189 Ewell Rd, tours/tastings roughly 12/2–9pm depending on day, second smaller taproom near Williamsburg Premium Outlets) or The Virginia Beer Company (401 2nd St, roughly Mon–Thu 3–9pm, Fri–Sat 12–9:30pm, Sun 12–7/9pm seasonal). Or head back into extended evening programming at Colonial Williamsburg.
Lodging — Marriott / Bonvoy Lens
Filtered for the household's usual pattern — Marriott Bonvoy, Marriott Vacation Club, and Interval International points and redemptions.
Richmond Marriott Hotel
Marriott · Downtown Richmond
Skywalk-connected to the convention center, walkable to James River Park, and a short trip to Carytown.
Courtyard by Marriott Richmond Downtown
Marriott · Shockoe Slip, Cary Street
≈2.3 miles from VMFA; per Marriott's own listing, within walking distance of Carytown.
Williamsburg Lodge
Strong Pick
Autograph Collection (Marriott) · Inside Colonial Williamsburg
A historic 1939 Rockefeller-era lodge, still owned and operated by the Colonial Williamsburg Foundation's for-profit arm, now part of Marriott's Autograph Collection. Sits inside Colonial Williamsburg itself — extremely well-located — with two outdoor pools, an indoor lap pool, and an on-site spa.
Marriott's Manor Club at Ford's Colony
Marriott Vacation Club · ≈10–15 min from the Historic Area
A gated golf community with 1- and 2-bedroom villas. Good MVC/Interval International points redemption option, even without needing the extra space a family would want.

Correction — Not A Marriott Property Kingsmill Resort is sometimes assumed to be Marriott-affiliated (it was originally built by Anheuser-Busch), but it is not a Marriott property — it's operated by Xanterra Parks and Resorts. Worth ruling out explicitly given the household's Bonvoy focus.
