02 · Reading orders — genuinely competing
Sanderson's own answer: it doesn't matter which series you start with. Five valid entry points per his FAQ — Mistborn: The Final Empire, The Emperor's Soul, The Way of Kings, Elantris, Warbreaker. His one hard rule: never skip ahead within a series. He specifically flags The Alloy of Law as a weak entry point despite opening Mistborn Era 2 — it spoils Era 1. Text-confirmed — brandonsanderson.com FAQ

03 · What actually connects everything

All of Sanderson's adult epic fantasy shares one cosmology: a shattered god's power split into sixteen Shards, each invested in a different world. Every named magic system draws on the same substrate — Investiture — moving through three overlapping Realms (Physical, Cognitive, Spiritual). Rare points where a Shard's Investiture pools heavily, called perpendicularities, let people cross between worlds. Hoid, under one alias or another, has cameoed in every published Cosmere book to date. That's the whole shared skeleton — the full Shard-by-Shard detail, including one late-series reveal, is in the companion report.

Core structure text-confirmed — full roster and one Shard's current status rest on thinner ground, see full report §5

06 · The collector's bottom line
Dragonsteel-direct (dragonsteelbooks.com) behaves like Subterranean does for Bujold in your author tracker — direct-sale leatherbounds, capped 2/customer, signed copies do not restock once sold out. For some editions, direct is the only route at all.

Direct-first pattern: unique Dragonsteel editions and signed stock appear months before the Tor trade edition (e.g. Isles of the Emberdark: direct Jul 2025 → Tor trade Feb 2026). Treat every Dragonsteel-only item as time-sensitive.
B&N carries the standard Tor catalog broadly (incl. a Stormlight 1–5 mass-market box) and runs signed tip-in campaigns on major releases. Could not confirm any official B&N-exclusive Sanderson edition (sprayed edges, exclusive covers) — only fan-made Etsy/eBay listings surfaced.
