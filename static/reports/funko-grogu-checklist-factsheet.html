Sourcing Confidence — kept visible, not smoothed over
Well-Supported
The core Season 1–2 numbered lineup (#368–#498) is corroborated across multiple independent checklist sites (pop-figures.com, cardboardconnection.com, funkypriceguide.com) with consistent numbering. The Book of Boba Fett figures (#584, #587) and the Season 3 IG-12 Deluxe (#720) are confirmed via Entertainment Earth's own product pages. The 2026 movie wave's headline figures (#818, AT-RT #837) and pricing are confirmed via Funko's official Toy Fair 2026 reveal, reported by Laughing Place and corroborated by Collider and TechRadar.
Thinly Sourced / Unconfirmed
Grogu with Crest #819 rests on a single retailer search-result snippet (bigbadtoystore.com) that could not be directly fetched to verify — a lead, not a confirmed fact. "Grogu with Snack (Flocked)" and "Grogu in Pram" are confirmed real SKUs but their numbered-line Pop #s could not be matched. The #575 Macy's Parade entry may be a duplicate/data error vs. #474/#475. The Impressions #772 release year is inconsistent (2024 vs. 2025) across listings.
Not Found / Ruled Out
No Grogu-specific standard Pop! vinyl figure tied to Ahsoka or Skeleton Crew exists, because Grogu does not appear as a character in either show — consistent across every source checked, though this is a negative finding rather than exhaustive absence proof. No Funko Pop! Town set featuring Grogu was found.
Currency of This List
This research reflects the state of the Funko Star Wars line as of late July 2026. Funko continuously adds new SKUs, particularly around the recently released Mandalorian and Grogu film — treat this as a snapshot, not a permanently complete list.
