CW Historic Area

VerifiedLead
Verified: Sat Nov 14 free day, 10 AM–5 PM · Lead: hours, programs, regular pricessrc
  • A ticket is needed to enter buildings and see programs. Aidan (under 2) is free either way. Finn is 6, so he is $0 on the Sat Nov 14 free day.
  • Fri and Sun, 2025 prices (2026 table not found): adult $35, youth 6–12 $10, under 6 free; buying online takes a further 10% off. On Fri or Sun Finn is a youth ticket (ages 6–12): $10 at 2025 prices, unverified for 2026.
  • Suggested shape: walk Duke of Gloucester Street, watch the costumed interpreters, pick one or two open buildings, ideally a trade shop where Finn can watch things being made. Skip long sit-down tours with Aidan.
  • Not every building is open at once; a grand union flag at an entrance signals an open site. Pick up the weekly program guide at the Visitor Center.
Not found: Nov 13–15 program schedule, multi-day pass (only a $75 annual pass appears), parking and shuttle. Could not confirm the Governor's Palace, Capitol or trade shops are open in November. Strollers: one unverified blog claim says many buildings do not allow strollers inside. Bring a carrier or a stroller you can leave outside.
