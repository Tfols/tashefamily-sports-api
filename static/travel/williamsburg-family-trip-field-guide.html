VerifiedLead
Verified: Sat Nov 14 free day, 10 AM–5 PM; 2026 prices · Closed: Sun Nov 15 · Lead: regular hours, open buildingssrc
  • A ticket is needed to enter buildings and see programs. Finn (6): youth ticket $10 on Fri, $0 on the Sat Nov 14 free day, and Sun is closed.
