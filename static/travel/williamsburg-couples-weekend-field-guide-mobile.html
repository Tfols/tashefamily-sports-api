One Last Morning, Then Home

Everything today is genuinely optional — the household’s own research tags both morning candidates as options, not anchors, so no stop spine is forced here. Pick one or both by energy and drive timing, then a casual lunch before departure.

Morning / midday — pick one or both
Freedom Park
Verified
600 acres, ~2mi trail incl. 1mi ADA-accessible paved loop. Historical core: one of the nation’s earliest Free Black Settlements (1803–1850), three reconstructed cabins (Jackson, Brown, Lightfoot families), 18th-c. cemetery, Battle of Spencer’s Ordinary site. Strong, lesser-known fit for Ashe’s interest. Hours daily 7a–sunset, Interpretive Center 10a–5p. Guided Free Black Settlement tours only run specific fall 2026 dates (Sep 27, Oct 10, Nov 19, Dec 12) — none in summer.
Yorktown Battlefield / Colonial NHP
Verified
~15–20 min from Williamsburg. A genuine but modest nod to Tom’s German-history interest: Hessian troops fought at the 1781 Siege of Yorktown, Regiment von Bose defended Redoubt No. 9. No dedicated German-history exhibit on-site — a fact a ranger would surface, not a standalone destination.
Backup if plans or weather shift
York River State Park
Verified
A different flavor than the historic-site cluster — Chesapeake Bay estuarine reserve, multi-use trails, boat ramp, fishing, seasonal kayak rentals. Active: Virginia DCR held a public master-planning meeting in May 2026 about new facilities there.
Williamsburg’s by-category rail (bookstores, coffee, breweries/wineries, food) lives under Saturday — that’s the day with browsing time. Sunday’s stops above are historic-site and nature options, not shop/food picks.
