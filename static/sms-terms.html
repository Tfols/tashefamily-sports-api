1Program description

The Tashe Family Household Digest is a private, non-commercial messaging program operated by Tom Folsom for members of his own household. Messages contain that day's household calendar events, outstanding household to-do items, and the local weather forecast, along with occasional household notifications such as a reminder or an alert relevant to the family.

This program sends no marketing, promotional, or advertising content of any kind.

2Who receives messages, and how they consented

Messages go only to two adult members of a single household, both of whom gave express prior consent to receive them and whose numbers were entered directly by the operator. There is no public signup: no web form, no keyword opt-in, and no way for anyone outside the household to join. Numbers are never purchased, rented, or obtained from any list or third party.

3Message frequency

Recipients should expect approximately one message per day, generally in the morning. Additional messages may occasionally be sent when a household matter warrants it. Frequency may vary.

4Cost

The operator does not charge for these messages. However, message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile plan. Any such charges are between the recipient and their mobile carrier.

5How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message from this program to cancel. A single confirmation message will be sent acknowledging the request, after which no further messages will be sent to that number.

A recipient who opts out and later wishes to resume can ask the operator directly to be re-added.

6How to get help

Reply HELP to any message from this program to receive contact information. Help is also available by email at tom.folsom@gmail.com.

7Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is dependent on carrier networks and is not guaranteed. Recipients should not rely on this program for time-critical or emergency information.

8Supported carriers

Messages are supported on major U.S. carriers, including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon Wireless, Sprint, U.S. Cellular, Boost, MetroPCS, and Virgin Mobile, as well as many smaller and reseller carriers. Carrier support may change without notice.

9Privacy

Information handling is described in full in the Privacy Policy. In short: phone numbers are used solely to deliver these messages, are never sold or shared for marketing, and are disclosed only to Twilio and the mobile carriers for the purpose of transmitting the messages.
