1Program description

Tom Folsom's Household Automation is a private, non-commercial scheduling and notification application built and operated by Tom Folsom. It runs on a fixed daily schedule with no human involvement in composing or sending any message: the application queries the Google Calendar API, the TickTick task API, and the National Weather Service API, assembles the results into a formatted summary, and delivers that summary by SMS to its registered recipients. It also sends automated alerts when conditions it monitors are met, such as a severe weather notice or an approaching deadline.

All messages are transactional system notifications. This application sends no marketing, promotional, or advertising content of any kind.

2Who receives messages, and how they consented

Messages go only to two adults, both of whom gave express prior consent and whose numbers were entered directly into the application's private configuration by the operator. There is no public signup: no web form, no keyword opt-in, and no way for anyone else to join. Numbers are never purchased, rented, or obtained from any list or third party.

Consent is obtained verbally and in person by the operator, who reads the following disclosure aloud and records the recipient's affirmative response along with the date:

Verbal opt-in script

"I've built an automated system that sends a daily text with our calendar, our to-do list, and the weather forecast. It's operated by me, Tom Folsom, and the messages come from my number ending in [last four digits].

It sends approximately one message per day, plus occasional automated alerts; message frequency may vary. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply. You can stop the messages at any time by replying STOP, or by telling me and I'll remove your number.

Do I have your permission to add your mobile number to receive these text messages?"

Consent is recorded only after an unambiguous yes. A recipient who declines is not added, and no message is sent to any number that has not gone through this process.

3Message frequency

Approximately one message per day, plus occasional automated alerts; message frequency may vary. The daily message is generally sent in the morning.

4Cost

The operator does not charge for these messages. However, message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile plan. Any such charges are between the recipient and their mobile carrier.

5How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message from this program to cancel. A single confirmation message will be sent acknowledging the request, after which no further messages will be sent to that number.

A recipient who opts out and later wishes to resume can ask the operator directly to be re-added.

6How to get help

Reply HELP to any message from this program to receive contact information. Help is also available by email at tom.folsom@gmail.com.

7Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is dependent on carrier networks and is not guaranteed. Recipients should not rely on this program for time-critical or emergency information.

8Supported carriers

Messages are supported on major U.S. carriers, including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon Wireless, Sprint, U.S. Cellular, Boost, MetroPCS, and Virgin Mobile, as well as many smaller and reseller carriers. Carrier support may change without notice.

9Privacy

Information handling is described in full in the Privacy Policy, which is the single authoritative privacy policy for this application.

Key privacy statement

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile phone numbers are used solely to deliver the messages described above. The only disclosure that occurs is the operational one necessary to send them: numbers are transmitted to Twilio, the licensed messaging provider carrying the messages, and onward to the recipient's mobile carrier for delivery. Twilio acts solely as a service provider and does not receive this information for its own marketing use.
