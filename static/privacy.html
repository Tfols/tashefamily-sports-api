Privacy Policy

tashefamily.com · Tom Folsom's Household Automation

SMS Privacy Notice. Tom Folsom's Household Automation collects mobile phone numbers and messaging consent only from recipients who expressly opt in to receive its automated household notifications. Mobile phone numbers and messaging consent are used only to provide the SMS messaging service.

We do not share, sell, or provide mobile phone numbers or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information may be provided to service providers, including Twilio and mobile carriers, only as necessary to deliver the SMS messaging service.

About this application. Tom Folsom's Household Automation is a private, non-commercial scheduling and notification application, built and operated by one person. It sends an automated daily schedule-and-weather summary by text message to two adult recipients who consented in advance. No one else can sign up, and nothing is collected from visitors to this website.

1Who operates this application

Tom Folsom's Household Automation is built and operated by Tom Folsom as a personal, non-commercial project. It is not a product, and it is not offered to the public. There is no signup form, no customer account system, and no way for a member of the public to subscribe.

Messages are generated and sent entirely by software on a fixed schedule. No person composes or sends any individual message.

2What information is collected

The application handles a deliberately small amount of information:

  • Mobile phone numbers of the two adult recipients. These are entered directly into the application's private configuration by the operator. They are not collected through any web form and are not obtained from any third party, list, or data broker.
  • Consent records noting that each recipient gave express prior consent, and the date it was given.
  • Message content, consisting of that day's household calendar events, outstanding household to-do items, and the local weather forecast.

No information whatsoever is collected from visitors to this website. There are no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies set by these pages.

3Where message content comes from

Digest content is assembled from services the household already uses and has authorized:

  • Google Calendar — read-only access to the household's own shared calendar.
  • TickTick — read-only access to the household's own task lists.
  • National Weather Service — a public government weather forecast for the household's area. No personal information is sent when retrieving it.

No mobile phone number, consent record, or other recipient information is ever transmitted to any of these services.

4How information is used

Mobile phone numbers are used for exactly one purpose: delivering the automated messages described in this policy to the recipients who consented to receive them.

Mobile phone numbers are not used for marketing, not used to build a profile, and not used for advertising or analytics. They are used solely to provide the SMS messaging service described in this policy.

5SMS messaging program disclosures

The following disclosures apply to the SMS messaging program covered by this policy:

  • Message frequency: Approximately one message per day, plus occasional automated alerts; message frequency may vary.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply. The operator charges nothing for these messages; any charges are between the recipient and their mobile carrier.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message to cancel. Opt-out is honored immediately and permanently unless the recipient later asks to be re-added.
  • Help: Reply HELP to any message, or email the address in Section 11.
  • Content: All messages are transactional system notifications. No marketing, promotional, or advertising content is sent.

Full program terms are published at /sms-terms, including the verbatim script used to obtain consent.

6Sharing and disclosure

No mobile information, including mobile phone numbers and messaging opt-in data or consent, is shared, sold, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile phone numbers are used solely to provide the messaging service described above. Mobile information may be disclosed to service providers, including Twilio and mobile carriers, only as necessary to transmit and deliver messages. Such service providers do not receive mobile information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Twilio handles this information under its own privacy policy at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law.

7How long information is kept

The application keeps no separate database of recipients or message history. Mobile phone numbers exist only in the operator's private application configuration and remain there until removed. Message delivery records are retained by Twilio according to Twilio's own retention practices.

8Security

Access credentials are stored as private environment variables on the operator's own machine and are not committed to any public code repository. Messages are transmitted to Twilio over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Plainly stated: standard SMS is not an encrypted medium, and no system is perfectly secure. Accordingly, message content is limited to routine household scheduling information and deliberately excludes financial details, account credentials, medical information, and similarly sensitive material.

9Your choices

Any recipient can stop all messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Opt-out is honored immediately and permanently unless that person later asks to be re-added. Replying HELP returns contact information.

A recipient may also ask the operator directly to remove their number, or to confirm what information is held about them.

10Children

Messages are sent only to adults. This application is not directed to children, and no information about children is knowingly collected through it.

11Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tom.folsom@gmail.com.
